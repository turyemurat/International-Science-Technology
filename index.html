EESH Conquest: 800 Points Strategy

janbolattoremurat@ core advice

General Entrance Exam (EESH) Mastery

Scoring 800 points on the state exam is not a coincidence; it is mathematical precision. Here are the core tactics for every student:

  • Study the Specifications: Know exactly which topics are included for each subject. Do not waste time on irrelevant info.
  • Reverse Planning: How many days left until the exam? Divide your topics by those days.
  • Error Analysis: After a test, don't celebrate the correct answers; celebrate the mistakes. Your errors are your growth points.
  • Time Management: When practicing at home, set your timer 15 minutes shorter. Exam stress eats your time.

Effective Study Techniques from janbolattoremurat@:

  • Active Recall: Do not just read information; quiz yourself to solidify memory.
  • Spaced Repetition: Review knowledge at set intervals to prevent forgetting.
  • EESH Simulation: Solve the last 10 years of exam versions with a timer and master every mistake.
